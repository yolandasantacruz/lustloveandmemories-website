# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

This website is a personal literary space dedicated to poetry, by Yolanda Santa Cruz. Your privacy and trust are deeply valued. This policy outlines how your interactions are gathered and protected.


### Information You Share

When you choose to use the **"Write a note"** form in the Correspondence section, you may share:

* **Your message or reflection:** The words, thoughts, or inquiries you choose to write.
* **Your email address:** Provided voluntarily so that Yolanda can read your reflection and reply to you directly.

This information is never used for automated mailing lists, newsletters, advertising, or promotional broadcasts.


### How Correspondence Is Honored

Any note and email address you send are received **exclusively to read your thoughts and respond**.

* Your correspondence is treated with the discretion of personal letters.
* Your details will **not be sold, rented, monetized, or shared** with third-party advertisers or data brokers.


### Analytics & Quiet Browsing

* **No invasive tracking:** This website does not employ commercial ad networks, behavioural tracking pixels, or third-party profiling cookies.
* **Clean performance:** Local browser preferences (such as reduced motion settings or session state) are respected natively on your device without tracking your identity.


### External Horizons

This site includes direct links to external platforms, such as Instagram ([@lustloveandmemories](https://www.instagram.com/lustloveandmemories)) and book distributors (such as Amazon). Please note that once you leave this website, third-party platforms govern their own independent privacy practices and terms.


### Personal Requests & Privacy

You may:

* Inquire about any note or message you have previously sent.
* Request the deletion of correspondence you have sent.

To request deletion, ask questions regarding this policy, or connect directly, please use the **Write a note** correspondence form on this website or reach out via Instagram at [@lustloveandmemories](https://www.instagram.com/lustloveandmemories).
